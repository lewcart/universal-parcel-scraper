# 2ebox

2ebox parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

2ebox, or 2ebox Casilla, gives shoppers in Chile a mailbox in Miami: its Miami warehouse receives
their purchases from shops in the United States and it delivers them in Chile. Its site is
2eboxcasilla.com.

## Numbers

Its tracking number, the guía, is emailed when a parcel reaches the Miami warehouse. A promotional
image of its tracker shows one, nine digits long. One example does not show how its numbers are
built, and other carriers' rules read nine digits, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form on its home page opens an order page that sent the one public example back to
the home page, so no reply has been seen. Customers also see their orders after logging in.

## Links

No address is known to open one parcel, so no link is read.
