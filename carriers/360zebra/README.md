# 360 Zebra

360 Zebra parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

360 Zebra (斑马物联网), based in Shanghai, runs cross-border lines, parcel forwarding and overseas warehouses,
including a small-parcel line from China to the United States. Its site is 360zebra.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page takes up to ten numbers separated by commas and looks them up without a login. No public
number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `trackingnbrs` on its tracking page.
