# 66Post

66Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

66Post (66Post INC) ships online orders from China to all fifty US states on its own direct
line, with delivery service partners for the last mile. Its site is 66post.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads numbers from its address and draws its answer on the server without a
session, leaving the result empty for an unknown number. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `numbers` on the tracking page.
