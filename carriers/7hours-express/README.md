# 7 Hours Express

7 Hours Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

7 Hours Express, founded in 2019 and based in San Dimas, California, delivers parcels in the Los
Angeles area the same day or the next with its own fleet. Its site is 7hoursexpress.com.

## Numbers

Its tracking numbers are record ids of about ten letters and digits in mixed case, without
structure, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker asks an open JSON service with an application id its page gives every visitor, and the
service answers an unknown number with an error. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply
yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `objectId` on the tracker.
