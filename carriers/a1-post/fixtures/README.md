table and the page's script: one row per scan, newest first, each a clock and a line of text.

`partner.html` is the current layout: a first row without a clock linking to USPS's tracker with
the number, then A1 POST's own two rows. `relayed.html` is the layout archived lookups show:
Bulgarian office scans followed by their office, A1 POST's rows in their earlier wording and
USPS scans followed by a US place and ZIP code, here ending in a delivery. One office scan falls
in the hour Sofia's clocks repeat and is listed twice. `not-found.html` is the page for a number
A1 POST does not know.

Numbers, places, offices and clocks are invented.
