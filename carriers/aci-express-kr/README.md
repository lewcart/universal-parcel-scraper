# ACI Express

ACI Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

ACI Express (에이씨아이월드와이드) is a Korean international courier. Its e-commerce service flies
parcels from abroad to Korea, clears them through customs and hands them to Korea Post, whose
thirteen-digit registered number the e-commerce tracker takes. Archived lookups show that
layout with ACI's own pickup, flight and customs scans. Those numbers stay with Korea Post's
rule.

ACI's delivery tracker also shows its own twelve-digit bill numbers for parcels it exports.
Twelve digits are a shape many carriers share, so no rule claims them.

## Links

The delivery page's e-commerce lookup opens `acieshop.com/aci_pod/pod.php?BL={number}`.
Archived lookups use the older `acieshop.com/pod.html?OrderNo={number}` and
`www.acieshop.com/trackingbl.html?bl={number}`, and bill numbers open at
`wms.acieshop.com/comn/deliveryTrack/{number}`. The courier lookup opens
`133.acieshop.com/webpod.php?HawbNo={number}` over plain HTTP.
