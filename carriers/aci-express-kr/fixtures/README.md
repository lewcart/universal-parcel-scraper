# Fixtures

The pages follow `www.acieshop.com/pod.php?OrderNo={number}`, with its malformed markup and
unclosed tags. `delivered.html` has ACI's pickup without a time, the flight, the arrival at
Incheon and customs, then Korea Post's scans under a thirteen-digit number: one arrival is
repeated, the postman is named, and a last line names the recipient and who took the parcel.
`delivered-cj.html` has the same milestones under a twelve-digit number, then CJ Logistics'
Korean scans, which start with a row without wording and name a driver in a branch.
`in-transit.html` has arrived at Incheon, with Korea Post's table still empty. `not-found.html`
is the page for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers, names, places and clocks are invented.
