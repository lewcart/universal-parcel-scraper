# Fixtures

`delivered.json`, `failed-attempt.json` and `not-found.json` follow the shape of the parcel
search's reply to the Cypriot site: the parcel's summary status, its branches, masked names and
notes, and every scan with its branch, wording and wall clock. `compact.json` follows the
shortened history the Greek site gets instead, with its milestones and the ones still to come.
Numbers, names, branches, phones and clocks are invented. `not-found.json` is the reply for a
number ACS does not know.
