# AGT Kurye

AGT Kurye parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

AGT Kurye Hizmetleri A.Ş., in business since 1993, delivers bank cards, toll cards, e-signature kits
and online orders across Turkey through about seven hundred agents, with same-day delivery. Its site
is agtkurye.com.tr.

## Numbers

Its numbers depend on the sending company, ten to thirteen digits, shapes many other networks print,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker needs the sending company, the number and the first letters of the recipient's surname,
behind a CAPTCHA.

## Links

The tracker has no per-shipment address, so there are no tracking links.
