# AHL Courier

AHL Courier parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

AHL Courier (Pvt) Ltd, also known as Ahlogistic, is based in Lahore and delivers online shops'
orders across Pakistan, cash on delivery and the same day or the next, as well as documents, gifts
and bulk shipments, and offers warehousing and fulfilment. Its site is ahlcourier.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts the number in a form with a session token, without a CAPTCHA, and answers an
unknown number with a message on the form page. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply
yet.

## Links

The tracker posts the number and has no address for one parcel, so there are no tracking links.
