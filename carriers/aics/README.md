# AICS

AICS parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

AICS Logistics Pvt. Ltd., formerly Arihant International Courier Service, is an international
courier and consolidator in Surat, India, that sends shipments abroad and hands them to partner
networks for delivery. Its site is aics.in.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page asks an open JSON service with a login its page gives every visitor, and the
service answers an unknown number with a not-found code. Its result names the partner that delivers
and the partner's number. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet. An adapter can
follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `tracking_number` on the tracking page.
