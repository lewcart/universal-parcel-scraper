# Fixtures

The pages follow the EMS Champion Post Algérie tracker's page for one number, without most of the
site's frame: a heading naming the number, then its scans newest first, each with a wording, an
office and a date.

- `inbound-delivered.html` is an EMS item from abroad, delivered after a failed attempt, with the
  sending post's scans and the notice the page adds above a delivered EMS item. Its dates carry a
  clock, and one scan is listed twice.
- `parcel-network.html` is an item of Algérie Poste's own on its parcel network, out of customs and
  on its way, whose dates are days only. One scan is listed twice, a row has no wording, and the
  page leaves its timeline unclosed, as the tracker's does.
- `unknown.html` is the tracker's answer for a number it holds nothing for: the heading over an
  empty timeline.

Numbers, offices and clocks are invented.
