# Atlantic International Express

Atlantic International Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no
dedicated adapter.

Atlantic International Express is an international courier based in Mumbai, India. It hands
shipments to a partner abroad and shows the partner's forwarding number next to its own.

## Numbers

Lookups archived from its tracker show six- and seven-digit waybills. Such short digit runs
are printed by many carriers, so no rule claims them.

## Links

The site builds `/track/<number>/` addresses on `atlanticcourier.net` for one shipment. The
former tracker took the number as `awbno` on `track.php`.
