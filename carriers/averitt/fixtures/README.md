# Fixtures

`delivered.html`, `in-transit.html` and `aged.html` follow the shape of the page
`tools.averitt.com/servlet/rsoLTLtrack` renders for one PRO number: the status, the PRO number
and service unit, the pickup, shipment, delivery and appointment details, and the shipment
progression. `in-transit.html` is a shipment still on its way, modeled on a delivered page.
`aged.html` is an older delivered shipment whose progression the page no longer shows.
`not-found.html` is the results page for a number without a shipment. PRO numbers, parties,
places, references, weights and clocks are invented, and the site around the tracking is cut
down.
