# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` are the tracking page for one shipment, trimmed to its
`<main>`: the status over the number, the progress rail, the route, the masked parties and the
timeline. `delivered.html` goes from the creation to the delivery, with who took the parcel after
the delivery's wording and two scans on the day of the delivery. `in-transit.html` has been
received at a hub, with the older wording for the creation and steps of the rail still to come.
`not-found.html` is the answer for a number the tracker does not know, under the search form
that keeps it.

Numbers, names, places and days are invented. Scripts are removed and icons emptied.
