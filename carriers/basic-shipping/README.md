# Basic Shipping

Basic Shipping parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Basic Shipping is the tracker of Basic 3PL, Inc., a US e-commerce shipping and fulfilment company
with its own fleet, which hands some parcels to other carriers for the last mile. Its sites are
basic3pl.com and basicshipping.info.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its terms of use forbid automated access.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `numbers` on the tracker's `/Track/info` page.
