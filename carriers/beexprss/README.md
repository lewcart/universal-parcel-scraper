# BeeXprss

BeeXprss parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

BeeXprss Courier Service, run by UMG Logistics, delivers documents and parcels to more than 200
cities across Myanmar from its head office in Yangon. Its site is beexprss.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its waybill numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads the number from its address and draws its answer on the server without a
session. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `tracking_name` on the tracking page.
