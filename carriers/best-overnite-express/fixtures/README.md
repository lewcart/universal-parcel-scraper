# Fixtures

The files follow the reply of `api/Tracking/GetTrackingDetails` on
`trackingprod.bestovernite.com` for one PRO number, with the parties, references and status
line the service sends and the scans oldest first. `delivered.json` is a shipment within
California in winter time, picked up on a day without a time and delivered with a receipt whose
place is who signed. `in-transit.json` is a shipment from California to Arizona that has been
picked up, with the day the service estimates for its delivery. `not-found.json` is the answer
for a PRO number the service has no shipment under.

Numbers, names, places, clocks and references are invented.
