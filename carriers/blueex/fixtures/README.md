# Fixtures

The files follow the reply of `trackall.php` for one number: a block with the number, the
progress rail and the scans oldest first. `delivered.html` goes from the booking to a delivery
that names who took the parcel, with a station scan listed twice, a failed attempt, and the last
out-for-delivery scan in the same minute as the delivery. `in-transit.html` is on its way to
the destination city. `no-history.html` is the tracker's answer to a number it holds no scans
for.

Numbers, names, cities and clocks are invented.
