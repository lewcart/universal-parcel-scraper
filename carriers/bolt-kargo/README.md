# BOLT Kargo

BOLT Kargo parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

BOLT Kargo Lojistik A.Ş. carries parcels for businesses and online shops across Türkiye's 81
provinces, including oversized items, hanging garments and business-to-business freight. Its site is
boltkargo.com.tr.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the recipient's phone number.

## Links

Its tracking addresses also carry part of the recipient's phone number, which a lookup needs, so
there are no tracking links.
