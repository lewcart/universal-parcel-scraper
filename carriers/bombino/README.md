# Bombino Express

Bombino Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Bombino Express is an Indian international courier with offices in Mumbai, Dubai, London and
New York. Some last legs go to postal or express partners, whose numbers the tracker shows
alongside.

## Numbers

Bombino air waybills are plain runs of seven to ten digits on its own tracker, in archived
lookups from its older pages. No rule claims them, since many carriers print digit runs of
these lengths.

## Links

Bombino's track page opens one or more air waybills at `www.bombinoexp.com/track?tracking_no=`
followed by the number; its search box sends `awb`. Older pages used `Tracking.aspx?txtAwb=`
and `track.aspx?AwbNo=`.
