# BQC Logistics

BQC Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

BQC Logistics (百千诚物流), based in Shenzhen, is an agent for express networks from Hong Kong and runs air and
sea lines with duties paid. Its site is 1001000.com.

## Numbers

Parcels sent through an express network carry that network's number. No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker looks up to ten of its own numbers up without a login or CAPTCHA. No public number is known to
see a found reply.

## Links

Its tracker looks numbers up in place, so no address names one.
