# Branding Worldwide

Branding Worldwide parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Branding Worldwide Pty Ltd, founded in 2009, has its office in Sydney, a warehouse in Melbourne
and offices in China and Hong Kong. It carries door-to-door courier, air and sea shipments from
China and Hong Kong to Australia, and also sells DHL Express services. Basl Express is its older
tracking site. Its site is brandingworldwide.com.au.

## Numbers

Its tracker shows a booking reference of its own and, for shipments handed to a partner such as
DHL Express, the partner's waybill number, which that carrier's rules read. No public page shows
one of its own references or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads the number from its address and embeds the scans in the page, without a
session or CAPTCHA. No public number has shown a known shipment's reply yet. An adapter can
follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `key` on the tracking page. Basl Express's older page posts
numbers from its form and has no address for one shipment.
