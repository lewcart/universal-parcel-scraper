# Braspress

Braspress parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Braspress Transportes Urgentes Ltda., founded in 1977 and based in Guarulhos, carries part loads and
parcels, including online orders, across Brazil from over a hundred branches of its own, and to
Argentina and the rest of Mercosur through Braspress Transportes International. Its site is
braspress.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a CPF alone, or a CNPJ with the merchant's order or invoice number, and has no
field for Braspress's own transport documents. Its pages give no layout of a carrier number, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs a CPF or CNPJ.

## Links

Lookups need the tax id, so there are no tracking links.
