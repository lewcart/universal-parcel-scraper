# Fixtures

`cleared.json` and `notified.json` follow the shape of the tracking service's reply: one row per
milestone, oldest first, each naming its shipment and carrying English and Spanish wording, a Madrid
wall clock, a type and an icon. `cleared.json` is a parcel through customs and out to its delivery
carrier in winter; `notified.json` is a parcel whose data alone has arrived in summer. Numbers and
clocks are invented. `not-found.json` is the service's fixed answer for a number it does not know.
