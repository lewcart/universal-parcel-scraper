# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` follow the shape of the page `rastrear.html?guide_numbers=`
renders for one talón: the talón and service over the progress rail, the origin and destination
with the sender, the recipient and who took the delivery, a commented-out line with the last
event, and the history, newest first. `delivered.html` runs from the documentation to a delivery
row without status or branch, as the page writes it, with a carta porte recovered at the
destination office ahead of the freight. `in-transit.html` stops at the destination's
distribution centre, with the rail's last steps pending. `not-found.html` is the notice for a
talón the tracker does not know.

Talones, parties, places and clocks are invented, and the site around the tracking is cut down.
