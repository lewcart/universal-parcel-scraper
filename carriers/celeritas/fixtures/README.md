# Fixtures

`transit.html` and `returned.html` follow the shape of the page Celeritas's tracker renders for
one reference: the search form, the hidden notice, the details list, the heading over the scans
and the scans, newest first, each with a wording and an explanation. `not-found.html` is the
notice for a reference the tracker does not know and `search.html` the page without a lookup.
References, shops, order numbers, places, points, weights, dates and clocks are invented.
`returned.html` repeats one scan and lists a pending-receipt scan among the return's.
