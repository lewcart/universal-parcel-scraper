# Fixtures

Pages of `https://tracking.citymail.se/?search=`, in the shape the tracker serves.
`delivered.html` is a door delivery with a delivery photo and the recipient's postcode and
town, pre-advised during the hour Stockholm's clocks repeat in October. `in-transit.html` is
a parcel prepared for delivery, with an estimated delivery day. `pickup.html` is a parcel left
at a service point after a failed delivery, and `returned.html` one sent back because nobody
collected it there. `not-found.html` is the notice naming a reference the tracker has nothing
for.

References, senders, places, clocks and the photo address are invented.
