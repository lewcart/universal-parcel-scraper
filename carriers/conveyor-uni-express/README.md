# Conveyor Uni Express

Conveyor Uni Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Conveyor Uni Express, a company of Conveyor Group in Dhaka, is an international courier from
Bangladesh with offices in Hong Kong and Singapore. Its site is conveyoruniexpress.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a house air waybill number. No public page shows one or states their layout, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker shows a waybill's scans on a plain page without a login or CAPTCHA and answers an
unknown number with a not-found message. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `hawbno2` on the shipment page, with `route=1`.
