# Fixtures

`form.html` is the home page trimmed to its language switcher and the tracking form, whose
anti-forgery token `POST www.copacourier.com/Hawb/Hawb` needs. The other pages follow that
post's answer. `delivered.html` is a parcel from Costa Rica delivered in Havana: one transit
row is repeated whole, and the van, the delivery confirmation and the delivery share a minute
with the van listed last, as the tracker can list them. Its delivery row's detail line names a
recipient and an identity card. `in-transit.html` is a parcel from Panama's free zone that
stopped at Cuban customs, as the histories of parcels for Cuban provinces do. `not-found.html`
is the notice for a HAWB the tracker does not know.

HAWB numbers, tokens, cookies, names, the identity card, towns, weights and clocks are invented.
