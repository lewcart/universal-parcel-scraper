# Fixtures

`page.html` is a reduced tracker page: the code and year fields, the hidden token the page's
script posts with them, and the style of the captcha a security plugin adds to the site's other
forms. The JSON files follow the reply of `/wp-json/correos-api/enviosweb/`, newest scan first.
`delivered.json` comes from abroad through the office of exchange and customs to its delivery at
a post office, with one scan listed twice. `in-transit.json` is posted at one office and sent on
to another. `export.json` leaves Cuba, handed to customs for a country the reply names in place
of an office. `unknown.json` is the reply for a code the tracker holds nothing for in the year
asked, and `expired-token.json` its reply to a token it does not take.

Places, clocks and tokens are invented.
