# Servicios Postales del Ecuador

Servicios Postales del Ecuador parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated
adapter.

Servicios Postales del Ecuador (Empresa Pública de Servicios Postales del Ecuador), which replaced
Correos del Ecuador, is Ecuador's national post. Gintracom delivers its parcels to the door within
Ecuador. Its site is serviciopostal.gob.ec.

## Numbers

Servicios Postales del Ecuador gives items UPU S10 numbers ending `EC`, as its tracker's example
shows. Generic postal detection handles these numbers, so no rule for Servicios Postales del
Ecuador claims them.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is an ASP.NET form with a reCAPTCHA checkbox that takes up to five numbers. No public
number has shown a known item's reply yet.

## Links

The tracker posts its form and has no address for one item.
