# Fixtures

`form.html` follows the shape of the English results page of Cyprus Post's tracker before a
lookup: the site's search form and the tracking form with its honeypot pair and form token.
`collected.html`, `abroad.html` and `inbound.html` follow the shape of the results page for one
item: its number, its category and its history's six columns. Numbers, offices, facilities,
postcodes, signers and clocks are invented. `not-found.html` is the page for a number without
data and `expired.html` the page a results link shows once it has expired.
