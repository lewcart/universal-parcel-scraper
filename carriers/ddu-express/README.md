# DDU Express

DDU Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

DDU Express, run by DDU Shipping & Logistics in Dubai, is mainly a freight forwarder for air, sea
and land freight and customs clearance, and also sends international courier shipments and
parcels within the United Arab Emirates. Its site is ddu-express.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes air waybill and booking numbers. No public page shows one, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads the number from its address and draws its answer on the server without a
session. No public number has shown a known shipment's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `trackno` on the tracking page.
