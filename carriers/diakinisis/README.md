# Diakinisis

Diakinisis parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Diakinisis (Διακίνησις) is a Greek third-party logistics company that stores goods and delivers
them across Greece with its own trucks, including online orders, small or bulky, collected from
shops and taken to homes. Its site is diakinisis.gr.

## Numbers

Its track and trace page asks for an order or voucher number and, optionally, the recipient's
phone number. No public number is known, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

The track and trace page takes numbers typed into its form; there are no tracking links.
