# DIDA International Logistics

DIDA International Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated
adapter.

DIDA International Logistics (嘀嗒国际物流), based in Shenzhen, ships business and personal
parcels from China to Japan as an agent of Sagawa, Yamato and Japan Post. Its site is
dida-exp.com.

## Numbers

Its Sagawa line keeps one number from China to the door, which Sagawa's tracker also answers. No
public page shows one of its numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts numbers to a query page and answers without a session or CAPTCHA, marking an
unknown number as missing. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

The tracker posts numbers from its form, so there are no tracking links.
