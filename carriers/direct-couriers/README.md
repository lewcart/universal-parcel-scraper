# Direct Couriers

Direct Couriers parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Direct Couriers is an Australian same-day courier with its own drivers in Sydney, Melbourne,
Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide and Auckland. Its Direct Home service delivers online orders to
homes. It is a different company from Direct Freight Express.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a job number, a reference or an order number. No public source shows a whole
job number, so the corpus is empty and no rule claims a layout.

## Links

The tracker posts the number to its own lookup, so no address opens one job.
