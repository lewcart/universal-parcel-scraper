# DTD Worldwide Express

DTD Worldwide Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

DTD Worldwide Express (Pvt.) Ltd., based in Lahore, is a Pakistani courier that sends documents
and parcels abroad, handing them to partners such as DPD UK and UPS, and delivers within
Pakistan. Its site is dtd.com.pk.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker on portal.dtd.com.pk is a plain page that reads the number from its address without
a session or CAPTCHA, and says when it finds no shipment. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `tracking` on the portal's tracking page.
