# Fixtures

The pages follow those `dx-track.com` serves in the session its freight lookup opens, with
their view state cut out: the menu the lookup redirects to, which gives the planned delivery
day and the latest scan, and the tracking history its button opens, which gives the
consignment's details and its scans newest first.

- `delivered-menu.html` and `delivered-history.html` are a one-item consignment delivered the
  day after its despatch. The delivery scan names whoever signed.
- `in-transit-menu.html` and `in-transit-history.html` are a two-item consignment on its
  delivery vehicle, with a planned delivery day.
- `not-found.html` is the tracker's notice for an unknown number, a wrong postcode or an old
  delivery.

Numbers, references, names, places, weights and clocks are invented.
