# Fixtures

The pages follow Easy Mail's server-rendered tracker page, cut down to its search form, the
current status and the table of scans, newest first, with each row naming the voucher in the
twelve-digit form the tracker prints.

`delivered.html` goes from the sender's booking to the delivery across the end of summer time:
one departure is listed twice, a non-delivery reason is followed by a second round, and the
delivery's store carries a tooltip with a phone and an address over a masked recipient.
`in-transit.html` is out for delivery again after a non-delivery reason, with a parcel the sender
brought to a store and the older spelling of an arrival. `not-found.html` is the answer for a
voucher Easy Mail does not know, and `challenge.html` stands in for a server bot check.

Vouchers, names, phones, addresses, stores and clocks are invented.
