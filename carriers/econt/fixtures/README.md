# Fixtures

`delivered.json`, `ready.json` and `prepared.json` follow the shape of the reply from Econt's
trace service: the shipment's found flag, its summary status in Bulgarian and in Latin script,
the office storing it, and its scans with their kind, Bulgarian and Latin text and Sofia wall
clock. Numbers, names, offices, routes and clocks are invented. `not-found.json` is the reply
for a number the service does not know.
