# Fixtures

The files follow eCourier's tracking page, cut down to its tracking section. `delivered.html`
goes from the booking at a fulfilment centre to a delivery, with a hub scan listed twice, a
rider's phone number in one scan, and both ways the page writes a scan's detail lines.
`in-transit.html` is on its way to another city, with a two-digit hour and a scan without a
location. `not-found.html` is the page's answer to an ID it does not know.

IDs, names, addresses, branches, phone numbers and clocks are invented.
