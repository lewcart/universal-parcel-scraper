# Elianpost

Elianpost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Elianpost (易连供应链), based in Guangzhou, runs cross-border lines and parcel forwarding to Korea, Japan,
Southeast Asia, India and the Gulf. Its site is elianpost.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page posts up to ten numbers, one per line, without a login. No public number is known to see a
found reply.

## Links

Its tracking page looks numbers up by posting its form, so no address names one.
