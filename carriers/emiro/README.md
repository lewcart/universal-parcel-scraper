# Emiro

Emiro parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Emiro is a courier in the United Arab Emirates offering domestic and international express, freight
forwarding, e-commerce fulfilment and last-mile delivery. Its site is emiro.ae.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is a plain page that reads the number from its address without a session or CAPTCHA, and
says when it finds no tracking information. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.
An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `tracking_id` on the tracking page.
