# Endeavour Delivery

Endeavour Delivery parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Endeavour Delivery is the delivery service of Endeavour Group, the Australian drinks retailer,
and brings Dan Murphy's and BWS orders to customers' doors. Its site is
endeavourdelivery.com.au.

## Numbers

Its tracking page takes an order or consignment number. No public page shows one, so there is no
rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the recipient's surname or business name with the number, which a number alone
cannot supply.

## Links

The links in its delivery messages are personal, so no rule reads them. The tracking page takes
numbers typed into its form.
