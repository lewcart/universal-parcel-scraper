# Energo Logistic

Energo Logistic parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Energo Logistic is an Italian carrier that delivers household appliances and other bulky
goods for retailers and manufacturers, with delivery appointments agreed by phone.

## Numbers

Shipment numbers are `EN`, two digits, six digits and a final digit, printed with dashes, as
complaints addressed to Energo Logistic show. `energo-1` claims the dashed form. Without the
dashes the number is two letters and nine digits, which GLS Italy's low rule also reads as one
of its depot codes, so the undashed form stays unclaimed.

## Links

Energo's tracking page asks for the order number together with the customer's e-mail address
or mobile number, and shows the result in an embedded page of its logistics platform. No
address opens a parcel from its number alone.
