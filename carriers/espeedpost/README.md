# Espeedpost

Espeedpost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Espeedpost (易速国际物流), based in Shenzhen, sends online sellers' parcels through the small-packet services of
China Post, Hong Kong Post, PostNL and other posts, through express networks and on its own lines to the United
States, Canada and Europe. Its site is espeedpost.com.

## Numbers

Parcels on a post's small-packet service carry that post's number. No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page takes up to 25 numbers, one per line, and shows their scans without a login. No public
number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `trackingnumber` on its tracking page.
