# Fixtures

The fixtures follow EURODIS's English tracking page, cut down to the page's main section.

`delivered.html` is a one-collo shipment sent from Austria and delivered in Hungary. Its
history repeats two of the collo's scans, the page names Express One as the member to contact
and gives the same number as the delivery partner's, and the rows the recipient's ZIP code
unlocks carry a sender and who took the parcel. `in-transit.html` is a two-collo shipment from
Spain to Germany still on its way: the shipment's history is empty, one scan names neither a
place nor a country, and the page names no member for the recipient yet. `not-found.html` is
the page for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers, names, places, contact details and clocks are invented.
