# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` follow the tracker's English page for one number: the
number in a hidden field and over the history, the port of loading, the local carrier's line,
the progress rail and the scans oldest first. `delivered.html` goes from registration in Korea
to a delivery by the local carrier, with a missed attempt before it, and links Sagawa's tracker.
`in-transit.html` has just reached Japan, repeats one scan and links Japan Post's tracker.
`not-found.html` is the tracker's whole answer to a number it has no record of.

Numbers, places, offices and clocks are invented.
