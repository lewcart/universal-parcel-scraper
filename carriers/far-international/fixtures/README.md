# Fixtures

The pages follow the results page for one number, cut down to its results.

- `delivered.html` is a parcel flown from Hong Kong and delivered by FedEx in the US, whose
  scans all state their offset. The summary still reads 在途 (in transit), the delivery names
  who signed, one scan is listed twice, one has no wording, two are listed out of order and the
  last-mile label is listed after the parcel reached the departure hub. The last-mile number is
  not FAR's own.
- `bilingual.html` is a parcel out for delivery whose scans have no zone and pair Chinese
  wording with its English translation.
- `no-history.html` is the answer for a number the tracker has nothing for.

Numbers, names and clocks are invented.
