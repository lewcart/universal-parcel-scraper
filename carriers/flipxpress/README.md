# Flip Xpress

Flip Xpress parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Flip Xpress, founded in Dubai in 2019, delivers parcels and online orders across the United Arab
Emirates and ships abroad, with pickup, warehousing and drop-off for online sellers. Its site is
flipxp.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page asks an open JSON service without a session or CAPTCHA, which takes several
numbers at once and answers an unknown one with an empty list. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

The tracking page keeps the number out of its address, so there are no tracking links.
