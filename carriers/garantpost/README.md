# Garantpost

Garantpost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

EMS Garantpost (ООО «ЕМС Гарантпост») of Moscow, founded in 1990, was Russia's national EMS operator
until 2004, when it handed that role to Russian Post. It has since been a private express courier for
documents and parcels across Russia and abroad through its own agents. Its site is garantpost.ru.

## Numbers

Its tracker gives GP or EG, nine digits and RU as its layout. That is the UPU layout Russian Post's
own numbers use, and no public Garantpost number is known, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is a form that posts the number with a token from the page, without a login or CAPTCHA,
and answers an unknown number with a not-found page. No public number has shown a known reply yet.

## Links

The tracker has no per-shipment address, so there are no tracking links.
