# Global Business

Global Business parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Global Business (全球快遞, Global Express Co., Ltd.), formerly 元本通運, is a Taiwanese courier in
the Global Business Group, whose main shareholder is Taiwan Taxi (55688). It runs same-day
couriers in Taipei and delivers parcels to homes across Taiwan with its own drivers. Its site is
global-business.com.tw.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the consignment note number printed on the waybill or the pickup order number
given when booking. No public page shows either, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is an ASP.NET form that posts the number back to itself without a login or CAPTCHA.
No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

The tracker posts its form, so no address names one shipment.
