Replies of the GLS group's recipient service, as GLS Poland's tracking page gets them, in the
shapes of its English `GROUP` path. Numbers, places, clocks and personal values are invented.

- `overview-delivered.json`: the anonymous `rstt029` overview of a delivered parcel: the full
  progress bar and the delivery time, no owner and no scans.
- `detail-delivered.json`: the postcode-gated `rstt028` detail of the same parcel, from the
  sender's data to the delivery, with a signature, recipient, street, postcode, reference and
  phone the result must not keep.
- `overview-in-transit.json` and `detail-in-transit.json`: the same pair for a parcel on its
  way, after its handover to GLS.
