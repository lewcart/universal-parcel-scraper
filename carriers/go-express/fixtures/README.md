# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `transit.html` follow the shape of the HTML fragment GO!'s tracking
service returns in English for a known waybill: the waybill and current status, the progress
rail, the responsible station's contact details and postcode form when the page shows them, the
history newest first and the link to the shipment's page. `delivered.html` lists its delivery
twice, as the live page does. `not-found.html` is the notice for a waybill the service has no
shipment for. Waybills, stations, hubs, contact details and clocks are invented.
