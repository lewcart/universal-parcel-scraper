# GO Logistics

GO Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Go Logistics Pty Ltd, in Camellia near Sydney, has been a business courier since 2005. It delivers
the same day across Sydney, the Central Coast, Wollongong and the Blue Mountains and overnight to
Newcastle, Canberra and regional New South Wales, sells prepaid coupons and stores goods for its
clients. Its site is gologistics.com.au.

## Numbers

Its coupon and consignment numbers are ten digits in the one public example. Ten digits are a shape
that many other carriers' rules and samples also read, so no rule claims it.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is a plain page, without a session or CAPTCHA, that lists a consignment's scans with the
driver and the delivery signature, dated in Sydney's clock without an offset. It answers an unknown
number with the heading alone. It keeps scans for years. An adapter can follow, and must leave out
the signatory's name and the signature.

## Links

The tracker's frame takes the number in the `item` parameter.
