# GoFast

GoFast parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

GoFast (够快供应链), founded in Dallas and now based in Guangzhou, ships online orders from China to
the United States: dedicated air and sea lines, small parcels, air express and freight to
marketplace warehouses. It clears them through its own US customs broker, stores them in its own
US warehouses and hands them to UPS, FedEx, USPS and other last-mile carriers or its own trucks.
Its site is gofastcn.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker is a tenant of the Hualei (华磊) forwarder platform, like
[Yingtu Supply Chain's](../yingtu/README.md): `trackIndex.htm` on gzgk.hailei2018.com reads numbers
from its address and draws its answer on the server without a session, leaving the fields empty
for an unknown number. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet. An adapter can
follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `documentCode` on the tracker.
