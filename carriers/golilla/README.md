# Golilla

Golilla parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Golilla S.R.L., set up with the Adeo group, Leroy Merlin's parent, delivers and installs bulky goods
such as pallets, stoves, air conditioners, doors, windows and furniture across Italy, including
Leroy Merlin's home deliveries. Its site is golilla.it.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its shipment numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker and the customer area are behind a bot check.

## Links

Links in its messages open the customer area with a token rather than the number, so there are no
tracking links.
