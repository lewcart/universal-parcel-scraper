# Happy-Post

Happy-Post parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

Happy-Post is a parcel-sending service run by Landmark Global, part of bpostgroup. A sender
drops the parcel at a Mondial Relay, Chronopost, Colissimo or Colis Privé point, or has it
collected, and that network carries it to Happy-Post's hub. The hub checks the parcel and
applies the final label of the delivering carrier. International parcels often leave as bpost
items issued through Landmark Global; others leave with carriers such as Deutsche Post,
Colissimo, DHL Express or MRW. The tracker shows that second number as the international
tracking number, and detection names the delivering carrier for it.

Happy-Post's own parcel numbers are runs of seven or eight digits. Mondial Relay, Colisweb,
Heppner and SEUR numbers share these shapes, so no rule claims them.

## Links

The French and English tracking pages on happy-post.com and the tracking page on
uk.happy-post.com open one parcel when its number is in the `id` parameter.
