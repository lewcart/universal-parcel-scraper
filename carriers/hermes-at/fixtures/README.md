# Fixtures

The pages follow the myHermes.at tracker's page for one number, without most of the site's
frame: the form with the number, the progress rail, the parcel's details and its scans newest
first, each with a time, a wording and a place after `Ort:`.

- `delivered.html` is a parcel posted at an Austrian PaketShop and collected at a post office
  after a failed attempt, in winter. One scan is listed twice, one place is `undetermined` and
  one PaketShop's number has lost its closing bracket, as on the tracker.
- `cross-border.html` is a parcel from abroad listed under Hermes's parcel number and Hermes
  Germany's H number, out for delivery in summer, with scans in Finland and Germany and
  Austrian Post's redirect banner for Hermes's number.
- `receiving-box.html` is a parcel under a twenty-two-digit number, left in a parcel locker in
  autumn, with Austrian Post's redirect banner for that number.
- `not-found.html` is the tracker's answer for a number it holds nothing for: the form and a
  message under it.

Numbers, places and clocks are invented.
