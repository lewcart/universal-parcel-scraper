# Hispapost

Hispapost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

Hispapost is a private postal operator in Spain. It delivers marketplace parcels to the
mailbox, the door or a collection point, as well as letters and council notifications. Its
tracker reads a parcel's barcode, which is the number the marketplace or its logistics partner
gave the parcel: AliExpress economy numbers (CNES and eleven digits), Joom numbers (BAE, five
digits, H and nine digits) and Temu numbers (BG). Customers quote them in complaints and
reviews about Hispapost, and Hispapost's replies name parcels by them. Other carriers deliver
the same families, and CACESA clears the AliExpress and Joom ones through customs, so no rule
claims them for Hispapost.

Hispapost says AliExpress economy parcels have no tracking after customs, because AliExpress
does not register them with it.

The number of a delivery attempt for a council notification is kept in quarantine, since the
source does not say what the identifier is.
