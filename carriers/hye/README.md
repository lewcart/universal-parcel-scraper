# HYE International Logistics

HYE International Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

HYE International Logistics (环洋国际), based in Jiaxing, Zhejiang, holds an international express licence,
ships parcels from China, mainly to the United States and Vietnam, and delivers across the United States. Its
site is hyexp.net.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker on tms.hyexp.net takes several numbers without a login. No public number is known to see a found
reply.

## Links

Its tracker looks numbers up in place, so no address names one.
