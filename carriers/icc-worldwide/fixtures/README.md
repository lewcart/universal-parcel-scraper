# Fixtures

`not-found.html` is the page `www.iccworld.com/track-result.php?track_no=` renders for a number
without a shipment: the shipment summary with its "Record not found" notice and no number, the
empty detailed report, the banner and the tracking form, with the site around them cut down.
`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` fill the same page in as the older tracker's page laid a
shipment out: the number over the summary, the origin, the destination, the receiver's name and
the delivery's day and time once delivered, a partner's number linked under them, then the
customer, the consignee and the checkpoints newest first. `delivered.html` is a shipment from New
York delivered in Mumbai through Blue Dart; `in-transit.html` one from London held at customs in
New Delhi, linked to a DHL waybill, with a row without a time. Air waybills, partner numbers,
names, places, wordings and clocks are invented.
