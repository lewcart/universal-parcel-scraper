# Indah Logistik

Indah Logistik parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Indah Logistik, also called Indah Cargo, carries parcels and cargo by land, sea and air between
its branches across Indonesia and delivers them to the door.

## Numbers

A resi starts with the sending branch's three-letter code, then `1CS` and eight or twelve
digits; older numbers have eight. The carrier's own resi page also showed a twelve-digit number
with `PL` after `CS`. These come from the carrier's archived resi page and from customer
questions about Indah Logistik parcels. `indah-logistik-1` claims these layouts at high
confidence: no other carrier's numbers or rules use them. Shorter examples from a news guide
stay unclaimed until the tracker shows them.

## Links

`indahonline.com/services/view` opens one resi from its `NO_RESI` parameter. The resi form
also asks for the last four digits of the recipient's phone and a captcha.
