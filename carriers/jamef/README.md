# Jamef

Jamef parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Jamef Transportes Ltda, founded in 1963 and based in São Paulo, carries part loads and parcels for
businesses and online shops across Brazil by road and air. Its site is jamef.com.br.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the invoice (nota fiscal) number with the recipient's or payer's CPF or CNPJ.
Jamef's own transport documents, CTRCs, have nine-digit numbers that go with a branch code and a
series. Its former tracker took them; today's does not. The only public CTRC comes from an archived
lookup on that former tracker, and nine digits are a shape many carriers' rules read, so no rule
claims it.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs an invoice number and the recipient's or payer's CPF or CNPJ.

## Links

Lookups need the tax id, so there are no tracking links.
