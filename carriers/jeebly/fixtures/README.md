# Fixtures

The pages follow the HTML of Jeebly's tracking page, `/shipment-tracking/`, after a lookup under
its Scheduled service, cut down to the form, the result and a contact field. `delivered.html`
goes from the order's data reaching Jeebly to the delivery: a failed attempt whose line carries a
phone number, a new delivery date and an edit of the order come before the second round, and the
pickup is repeated. `in-transit.html` is out for delivery under a `JBP` number. Both carry pickup
and drop-off cards and the package card. `not-found.html` is the page's answer for a number the
service does not know.

Numbers, names, addresses, phone numbers, places and clocks are invented.
