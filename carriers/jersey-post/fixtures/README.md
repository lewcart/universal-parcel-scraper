# Fixtures

The replies of the tracking page's lookup, `/Umbraco/Surface/Tools/Track/`, for one number, newest
scan first, with the carrier block the page reads. `delivered.json` goes from the sender's
announcement in Jersey to a delivery abroad: Jersey's own scans have no place and clocks to the
second, the relayed ones a country; one relayed scan is repeated, a failed attempt gives its
reason after a dash, and the proof of delivery names who signed. `in-transit.json` is an item held
by customs abroad, relayed for a number of the untracked service. `not-found.json` is the answer
for a number the tracker has no information on.

Numbers, names, clocks and the places abroad are invented.
