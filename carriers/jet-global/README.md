# Jet Global Logistics

Jet Global Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Jet Global Logistics (捷通物流) runs road freight between Hong Kong, Zhuhai and Macau, warehousing and local
delivery in Macau and Hong Kong, and delivers online orders. Its site is jetft.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker reads the numbers from the address and shows the delivery status without a login. No public
number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `order` on its `tracking.html` page.
