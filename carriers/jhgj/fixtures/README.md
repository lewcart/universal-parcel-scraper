Synthetic replies of Jiuhao International's Hualei tracker: `track.json` is the interface's
record for a script written in one go at the order's creation, from the collection in Guangzhou
to the delivery round, whose rows from China carry their place in brackets and whose destination
rows end with the recipient's town and postcode; `track-delivered.json` is the same record once
the delivery row was added, with a signatory; `page.html` is the tracking page for an order
without scans found by the shipper's reference; `no-track.json` is the interface's answer for a
number it holds no row for, and `page-unknown.html` the page's answer for an unknown number.
Numbers, references, parties, places and clocks are invented.
