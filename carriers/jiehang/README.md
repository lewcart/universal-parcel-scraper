# Jiehang International Logistics

Jiehang International Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated
adapter.

Jiehang International Logistics (杰航国际物流), based in Shenzhen since 2005, ships online
sellers' orders from China to their buyers on its own lines, takes stock to Amazon's warehouses,
resells international express and runs overseas warehouses. Its site is jiehang.net.

## Numbers

Its tracker shows its own waybill number and the number of the carrier that delivers the last
leg. No public page shows one of its numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts numbers from its form and loads the scans in a second request, without a
session or CAPTCHA. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

The tracker posts numbers from its form, so there are no tracking links.
