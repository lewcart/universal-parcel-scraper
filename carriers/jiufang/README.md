# JiuFang Logistics

JiuFang Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

JiuFang Logistics (九方通逊), based in Shenzhen, runs air and sea lines to marketplace warehouses and
small-parcel lines to the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany and the European Union. Its site is
jiufanglogistics.cn.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its shipment query posts the numbers without a login and lists their scans. No public number is known to see
a found reply.

## Links

Its shipment query looks numbers up in place, so no address names one.
