# KE 嘉里快遞

KE 嘉里快遞 parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

KE 嘉里快遞 (Kerry Express Co., Ltd.), founded in 1984, delivers express parcels and home deliveries in
Taiwan from its own branches along the west coast, and reaches the rest of the island through the
network of [Kerry TJ](../kerry-tj/README.md), whose group it joined. It also carries valuables and
cash-on-delivery parcels and runs a motorbike courier in cities. Its site is kerryexpress.com.tw.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes waybill numbers of eight digits, alone or after 00 or 436; the last has the shape
of Kerry TJ's numbers too. Many carriers' rules read these shapes, and no public number is known, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is behind a CAPTCHA.

## Links

The tracker posts the number from its form, so there are no tracking links.
