# Kuayuan Express

Kuayuan Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Kuayuan Express (跨源速递), run by Shenzhen Kuayuan Express Supply Chain, ships online orders from China to
the United States and Europe, clears them through customs and delivers them with its own vehicles. Its site is
kuayuanexpress.com.cn.

## Numbers

Its tracker's page shows one example, a digit and three letters followed by ten digits, which the tracker
answers with its scans. One example does not show how its numbers are built, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker on its home page takes up to 20 numbers and looks them up through its own API without a
login.

## Links

Its tracker looks numbers up in place, so no address names one.
