# Kuehne+Nagel

Kuehne+Nagel shipments use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

Road shipments on Kuehne+Nagel's public tracker carry a two-digit year, `A` and nine digits.
`kuehne-nagel-1` suggests Kuehne+Nagel at low confidence. The tracker also resolves hyphenated
customer references and shows ten-digit house air waybills. A road number starting `RWM` has
three letters and nine digits, the shape Purolator's rule owns.

## Links

The home page's tracking field links to
`mykn.kuehne-nagel.com/public-tracking/shipments?query={number}`.
