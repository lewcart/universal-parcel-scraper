Synthetic replies of Kun Express's Hualei tracker: `track.json` has the forwarder's own rows,
written to the second from the order data to the destination airport, then the rows it copies
from USPS, to the minute, with their doubles: alone, after their place and with USPS's
explanation. `no-track.json` and `page-unknown.html` are the answers for an unknown number.
Numbers, references, parties, places and clocks are invented.
