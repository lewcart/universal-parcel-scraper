# Fixtures

The pages follow the tracker's IPS Web Tracking item page in English, oldest scan first.

`outbound-delivered.html` is an item posted in Burkina Faso and delivered in France: its
posting office's name carries a postcode, one inward arrival is listed twice, two French scans
name their office by number only, and the delivery names a signatory and links an image.
`inbound.html` is mail from France at a Burkina Faso delivery office after a failed attempt,
whose extra information gives a reason, a measure and a signatory. `no-information.html` is the
page's answer for a code it holds nothing for.

Numbers, names, places and clocks are invented.
