# Fixtures

The pages follow `y-track.jp/ytrack/inqservlet?cd=81118&id=02116&number01={number}` and are
encoded in Shift_JIS, as the tracker serves them. `delivered.html` has the list row, the
details with the disabled drop-off button, and a history from the intake at a co-op centre
through two hand-overs to a member company and its delivery, with the offices' contact links.
`in-transit.html` is a parcel taken in on New Year's Eve and handed to a member company on New
Year's Day. `not-found.html` is the answer for a number the tracker does not know, with its
state in the list and no details.

Numbers, companies, centres, offices, links and clocks are invented.
