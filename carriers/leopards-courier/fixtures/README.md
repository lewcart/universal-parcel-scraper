# Fixtures

The pages follow Leopards' tracking page at `pk.leopardscourier.com`, cut to the parts the adapter
reads: the consignment number, the progress bar, a delivery box naming who signed, the booking
details and the scans newest first. `delivered.html` is a delivery whose wording names who took
the parcel, with a scan repeated. `in-transit.html` is a consignment on its way, with booking
details filled in. `pending.html` is a delivery left pending for a wrong address. `not-found.html`
is the lookup's reply for a number the site has no record of.

Numbers, names, cities, references and clocks are invented.
