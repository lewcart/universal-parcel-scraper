# LiBang International Logistics

LiBang International Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

LiBang International Logistics (立邦国际物流), based in Shenzhen, runs parcel and freight lines from China to
Taiwan, Hong Kong and Thailand, with cash on delivery in Thailand. Its site is lbexps.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its check page posts waybill numbers, separated by commas, to the site's WordPress handler without a login.
No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

Its check page looks numbers up in place, so no address names one.
