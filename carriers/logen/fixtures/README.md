# Fixtures

The pages follow `www.ilogen.com/web/personal/trace/{number}`: the parcel's details, then its
scans oldest first, with the staff, receiver, office and phone columns. `delivered.html` goes
from collection to delivery: one hub arrival is repeated, a bagging scan has an empty line, the
out-for-delivery scan gives a two-hour window and the delivery names who took the parcel.
`out-for-delivery.html` ends on an out-for-delivery scan with its window. `in-transit.html`
has left the pickup branch and reached a hub. `not-found.html` is the page for a number Logen
has no parcel for.

Numbers, names, phone numbers, places and clocks are invented.
