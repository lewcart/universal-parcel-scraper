# LogoiX

LogoiX parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

LogoiX in Freilassing gives private and business customers a delivery address in Germany,
forwards their parcels and books shipments with partner carriers across Europe. Its parcel
number is LIX followed by ten digits, seen in archived lookups on its tracker from 2010
onwards. `logoix-1` claims it with high confidence. The tracker also lists the number of the
transport partner, Austrian Post or DHL, which belongs to that partner.

## Links

The tracker opens one parcel from its `q` parameter, which the site's search form sends.
