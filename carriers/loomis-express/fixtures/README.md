# Fixtures

`in-transit.html`, `delivered.html` and `not-found.html` follow the shape of the results page
Loomis Express's tracker renders for one waybill: the list of waybills it found or could not
find, then for a found one its shipment and piece numbers, reference lines, the progress bar
and the scans, newest first, with the estimated delivery day on top while the parcel is on its
way. Numbers, references, places, dates and clocks are invented.
