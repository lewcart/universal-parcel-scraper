# Fixtures

The pages follow M&P's tracking page for one consignment, cut to its header search box, the
form with the booking details, the scans and the notification form, without the menus, scripts
and chat widget. `delivered.html` is a cash-on-delivery consignment whose delivery names who
took it, with one scan listed twice. `in-transit.html` is a twelve-digit consignment on its way.
`attempted.html` is a failed delivery attempt whose reason is followed by a line on the next
try, with a scan without a city. `not-found.html` is the tracker's answer to a number it has no
record of.

Numbers, names, cities, order references, form tokens and clocks are invented.
