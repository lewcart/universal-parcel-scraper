# Fixtures

The pages of `madhurcouriers.in/CNoteTracking`, cut down to the tracker's form and answer
area, with their hidden form values replaced.

- `form.html` is the page the tracker opens under its session: the C-Note form, with the empty
  booking details panel, the image sections and the history area.
- `not-found.html` is the answer for a C-Note the tracker holds nothing under: the form with the
  number left in its field and an empty history area, without any notice.
- `delivered.html` is the answer for a parcel booked in Samplepur, forwarded through Exampleganj
  and delivered in Testabad, the delivery sheet's rows naming the receiver and the staff.
- `in-transit.html` is the answer for a document booked in Samplepur and on its way.

Numbers, names, places, phones and days are invented.
