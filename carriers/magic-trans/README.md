# Magic Trans

Magic Trans parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Magic Trans (Мейджик Транс) is a Moscow road carrier for consolidated freight with terminals in over
320 Russian cities. It also carries private shipments and delivers to marketplace warehouses. Its
operator is ООО «МТ ЛОГИСТИКА». Its site is magic-trans.ru.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a nine-digit act number, with leading zeros, or a cargo code. The page gives an
act number as its example, and two cargo codes, TR, nine digits and MT, sit in its HTML comments.
Nine digits are a shape other carriers' rules take, and no shipment is known behind the cargo codes,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker answers only after a Yandex SmartCaptcha check.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `id` on the `otsledit-gruz` page at magic-trans.ru.
