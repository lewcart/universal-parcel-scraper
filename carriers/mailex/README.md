# Mailex

Mailex parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Mailex (매일택배), based in the Toronto area with branches across Canada, sends parcels from Canada to
Korea by air, where Korea Post delivers them. Its site is mailexglobal.com.

## Numbers

Its air waybills are eleven digits, printed in groups of four, two and five, a shape other networks
print, so there is no rule. In Korea the parcel travels on a Korea Post number.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker asks an open form service with the waybill alone, without a login or CAPTCHA, and its
reply gives the Korea Post number. No public waybill has shown a known reply yet. An adapter that
hands the parcel to Korea Post can follow.

## Links

The tracker has no per-shipment address, so there are no tracking links.
