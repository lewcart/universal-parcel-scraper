# Makati Express Cargo

Makati Express Cargo parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Makati Express Cargo carries balikbayan boxes and air cargo door to door from Gulf branches
and other countries to addresses across the Philippines.

## Numbers

The tracker takes the AWB number with the sending branch's three-letter code in front, as
Makati Express's support agents tell customers on its own forum. Its forum shows Dubai, Doha,
Fahaheel and Taif numbers with seven or eight digits after the code. `makati-express-1`
suggests the carrier for those four codes at low confidence. Australia Post's rule for three
characters and seven digits also takes the seven-digit ones. Other branch codes are not
claimed until numbers show them.

## Links

The tracking form posts the AWB to `makati-express.com/tracking/`, so no address opens one
parcel.
