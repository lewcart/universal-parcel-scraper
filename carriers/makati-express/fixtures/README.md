# Fixtures

The fixtures follow the page `makati-express.com/tracking/` returns after its form posts one
AWB, cut down to the form and the answer below it. `delivered.html` is an air cargo from a
branch on the older system: ISO days, four columns, a note on the departure, a phone number and
the receiver in the notes. `in-transit.html` is a box from a branch on the newer system: days
with their weekday, an office note column, a repeated row and two forecasts, the last of them
also the summary's status. `not-found.html` is the tracker's answer for an AWB no branch has
uploaded.

AWB numbers, names, places, notes, phone numbers and days are invented.
