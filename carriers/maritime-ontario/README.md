# Maritime-Ontario

Maritime-Ontario parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

M-O is a Canadian less-than-truckload and expedited freight carrier. Its quick-track form takes
a freight bill (pro) number, a bill of lading or a purchase order. M-O's Track and Trace
customer guide shows a freight bill as a capital F followed by seven digits. A single
documentation example is too little to claim the shape, so no rule claims it.

## Links

The quick-track form on `m-o.com` calls a public trace service from the page and has no
shareable result URL. Older trackers on `cp.m-o.com` and `customerportal.m-o.com` are gone,
and the `/di/` portal needs a login.
