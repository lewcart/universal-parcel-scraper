# Maruti Air Couriers & Cargo

Maruti Air Couriers & Cargo parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated
adapter.

Maruti Air Couriers & Cargo is a domestic courier founded in Mumbai. It carries documents and
parcels between its branches across India.

## Numbers

Maruti Air document numbers are plain runs of eight or nine digits in customers' complaints. No
rule claims them, since many carriers print digit runs of these lengths.

## Links

Maruti Air's tracking page frames a tracker on `erp.marutiair.com` that posts the number from a
form, so no confirmed address opens one parcel. The tracker's own address carries an `AWB`
parameter; the older site used `Doc_Tracking.aspx?No=`.
