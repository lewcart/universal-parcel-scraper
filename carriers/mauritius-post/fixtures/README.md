# Fixtures

The pages follow the tracking page's answer to `?tracking_code=`, cut down to the tracking form,
its results section and the help heading after it. `delivered.html` is a domestic item from the
customer's booking to its collection at the counter, with a scan repeated, two scans in the same
minute and the estimate and days in transit the header shows. `in-transit.html` is an item on its
way in the earlier wording, echoed in lower case in the form, with the empty estimate and the
"Invalid date" the header showed then. `not-found.html` is the notice for a number the tracker
holds nothing for.

Numbers, places and clocks are invented.
