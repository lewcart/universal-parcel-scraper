Synthetic replies of Mayi Yinfu's Hualei tracker: `track.json` is the interface's record for a
parcel in transit, with the forwarder's own rows, the rows coded `CO_C_CR` and rows relayed from
DHL after the hand-off; `track-delivered.json` is a shorter record that ends with DHL's delivery;
`no-track.json` is the interface's answer for a number it holds no row for, and
`page-unknown.html` the page's answer for an unknown number. Numbers, references, parties,
places and clocks are invented.
