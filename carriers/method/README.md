# Method

Method parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Method (METHOD Advanced Logistics) is a Spanish last-mile carrier with its own drivers and
depots. Its Method XL service delivers and installs bulky goods such as appliances and
furniture for retailers and marketplaces, and it also takes bulky shipments from individuals.

## Numbers

No public source quotes a Method tracking number, so the corpus is empty and no rule claims a
layout.

## Links

The shipment locator on `method-logistics.com` asks for the tracking number and, optionally,
the delivery postcode, and sends them in a form. No address opens one parcel.
