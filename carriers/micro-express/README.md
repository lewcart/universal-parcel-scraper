# Micro Express

Micro Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

Micro Express is an Australian last-mile courier with depots in Melbourne and Adelaide. Its
tracker asks for an ME tracking number, and customer reports give numbers of `ME`, thirteen
digits and three letters, which `micro-express-1` claims.

## Links

The tracker is a `#` route of a single-page app on `microexpress.com.au` and `mircoexpress.com`
that loads the history with a POST request, so no address opens one parcel.
