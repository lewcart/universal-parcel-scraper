# Mondial Relay fixtures

- `available-at-relay.json`: `/api/tracking` reply for a parcel waiting at its Point Relais (headline, two events, two milestones, an estimate, the relay's record on the arrival scan, recipient blocks). All values are made up; the relay's name and address are the projected pickup point, and the `PRIVATE …` placeholders are what the privacy assertion looks for.
- `inpost-es-awaiting-collection.html`: InPost Spain's tracking page for an `FV` number waiting at a Punto Pack, cut down to its two Astro islands (the search form and the tracking details, whose props carry the milestones and their coded scans, one of them twice). All values are made up.
