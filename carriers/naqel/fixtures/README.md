# Fixtures

The pages follow `www.naqelexpress.com/en/Track/TrackShipment/{number}`: the tracking form, the
shipment's summary and progress rail, then its scans newest first under day headings.
`delivered.html` is an import collected abroad, cleared and delivered through a parcel locker,
with scans the system records at SYSTEM. `in-transit.html` is a domestic shipment that has left
the hub, with one scan repeated. `not-found.html` is the page for a number Naqel has no shipment
for.

Numbers, places, clocks, the in-transit summary and the footer are invented.
