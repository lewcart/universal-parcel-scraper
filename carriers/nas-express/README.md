# NAS Express

NAS Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

NAS Express Couriers LLC (NASEX), based in Al Qusais, Dubai, collects and delivers documents and
parcels within the United Arab Emirates, often the same day, with e-commerce and cash on delivery
services. It also sends and receives international express shipments and air and sea freight.
Its site is nasxpress.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes air waybill numbers. No public page shows one or states their layout, so there
is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker on online.nasxpress.com is a form that posts the air waybill number back to its page,
which draws the answer on the server. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

The tracker ignores numbers in its address, so no address opens one parcel.
