# NOEST Express

NOEST Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

NOEST Express is an Algerian courier founded in 2018. It collects from merchants and delivers
across most of Algeria, to homes or to its stop-desk offices, with cash on delivery.

## Numbers

The one public NOEST tracking number found is three letters, three letters or digits and eight
digits, joined by hyphens. No rule claims that layout until more numbers confirm it.

## Links

NOEST's public tracker takes one or more numbers in a form and posts them, so no address opens
one parcel.
