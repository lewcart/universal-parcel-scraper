# Fixtures

The pages follow the shape `TrackingOnline/index` renders for one number: the search box, the
number, the timeline of events newest first, each with a heading, a day and a month, a clock and
a sentence, and the panel with the origin, the recipient, the delivery address and a map.

- `delivered.html` goes from the booking to the delivery, whose sentence names the recipient. A
  first centre scan reads twelve hours before the pickup listed under it.
- `in-transit.html` crosses New Year and is out for delivery again after a rescheduled delivery.
- `returned.html` has failed attempts, a delivery OCASA closed and the return; one attempt is
  listed twice.
- `not-found.html` is the notice for a number the tracker does not hold, with an empty panel.

Numbers, names, shops, addresses, centres, the map key and clocks are invented.
