# Om Logistics

Om Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Om Logistics Supply Chain Private Limited, in New Delhi, took over the logistics business of its
parent, Om Logistics Limited. It carries full and part truckloads and express consignments across
India by road, rail and air and on to Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar, and runs warehouses.
Its site is omlogisticssupplychain.com.

## Numbers

Its dockets are digits. Archived lookups on its former tracker show dockets of eight, nine, twelve
and thirteen digits. Other carriers' rules read each of those lengths, one of them at high
confidence, so no rule claims them.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is behind a CAPTCHA. The former tracker, which needed none, is gone.

## Links

Every lookup needs the CAPTCHA, so there are no tracking links.
