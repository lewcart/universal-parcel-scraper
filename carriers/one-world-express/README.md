# One World Express

One World Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

One World Express is a British cross-border parcel company. Its tracker, SmartTrack, also
answers for the partners that deliver its parcels, such as Yodel in the UK.

## Numbers

One World Express's own numbers are `OWE` and twelve digits. An archived SmartTrack lookup
shows one with its history, and `one-world-express-1` claims the layout. The example on its
tracking page has the layout of Evri's sixteen-character numbers, which Evri's rule already
covers, so it is kept only for review. Partner numbers stay with their carriers.

## Links

`smarttrack.co/tracking.php?tracking_number={number}` opens one parcel; One World Express
links SmartTrack as its parcel tracker. The tracking page on oneworldexpress.com loads its
result into the page without changing the address.
