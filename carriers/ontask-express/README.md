# Ontask Express

Ontask Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Ontask Express (安时达), run by 广州安时达国际供应链有限公司 in Guangzhou, ships goods from China to
Saudi Arabia door to door by air and sea, cleared through customs with duties paid at both ends,
and to the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Yemen. It carries e-commerce small
parcels, delivers to Amazon and noon warehouses in the Middle East, runs warehouses in Jeddah and
Riyadh and has a line to Mexico. Its site is ontaskd2d.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes tracking numbers separated by spaces. No public page shows one or states their
layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker on logistics.ontaskksad2d.com posts the numbers as JSON to an endpoint on the same
host and draws the reply in the page, without a session. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

The tracker reads no number from its address, so no address opens one parcel.
