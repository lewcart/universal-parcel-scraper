# Fixtures

`delivered.jsonp`, `handoff.jsonp` and `not-found.jsonp` rebuild the status service's
`callback(...)` replies with synthetic numbers, clocks and sorting-centre names. They keep
its envelope, its escaping, the oldest-first history with codes and short categories, and the
two wordings the tracker uses for the same codes. `handoff.jsonp` ends with a parcel handed to
another country's carrier under the code of a parcel waiting for pickup.
