# OTS Chile

OTS Chile parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

OTS Chile delivers online shops' orders the same day in 37 communes of the Santiago metropolitan
region, collecting them at midday, and connects with Mercado Libre and other sales platforms. Its
site is otschile.cl.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes the number from the order's confirmation e-mail or receipt, and the tracking
widget suggests six or more digits. Many carriers' numbers have that shape, and no public number is
known, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page embeds a third-party tracking widget, whose page for each shipment sits on the
widget vendor's domain and answers an unknown number as not found. No public number has shown a
known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

The tracking page reads no number from its address, and the widget's shipment pages are on the
vendor's domain, so there are no tracking links.
