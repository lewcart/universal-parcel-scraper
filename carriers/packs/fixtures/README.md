# Fixtures

The fixtures follow the page Packs' tracking form posts back to, `www.packs.nl/track-trace/`,
cut down to the result block and some of the page around it, which has headings of its own.

`delivered.html` goes from the announcement to a delivery after a failed attempt, with two
planning notices, and states the delivery above the rail. `in-transit.html` is in the sorting
process, with a status in the hour the clocks repeat when they go back, and an expected
delivery day and window. `registered.html` is announced only. `summary.html` is the page for a
sender's reference: a delivery stated above the rail with no status details, under a shipment
number that is not the one asked. `not-found.html` is the tracker's notice for a number and
postcode combination it does not know, and `form.html` is the empty form the page shows
without a lookup.

Numbers, postcodes, names, addresses and clocks are invented.
