# Paisley Freight

Paisley Freight parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Paisley Freight is a specialist courier in the United Kingdom for alloy wheels and tyres, bikes,
car parts, sporting goods and pallets, booked on its site. Its site is paisleyfreight.com.

## Numbers

Its tracking page asks for the booking reference: the booking's date and time as fourteen digits,
a hyphen, a letter and four digits. Only the page's made-up example is known, so there is no
rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking form posts the reference with a reCAPTCHA token.

## Links

The tracking page takes references typed into its form; there are no tracking links.
