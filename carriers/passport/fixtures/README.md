# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` follow the markup of Passport's merchant tracking page:
the estimate, the local delivery partner, the current status, the history grouped under its
milestones with a day, month and clock per scan, and the shipment the page hands its script.
One parcel is delivered by Royal Mail under an S10 number, with Royal Mail's wall clocks; the
other travels to Australia with Parcel Right over a new year, with an estimated row the page
fills in. Numbers, merchants, places, references and clocks are invented. `not-found.html` is
the notice for an unknown number.
