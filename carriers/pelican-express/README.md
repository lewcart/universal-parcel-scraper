# Taiwan Pelican Express

Taiwan Pelican Express is the Taiwanese door-to-door parcel network 台灣宅配通.
Its [public search](https://query2.e-can.com.tw/%E5%A4%9A%E7%AD%86%E6%9F%A5%E4%BB%B6A.htm)
asks for the first twelve digits of the waybill. A checksum shared by other national
networks does not establish Pelican ownership.

The guest detail page is `GET /self_link/id_link.asp?txtMainID={number}` on
query2.e-can.com.tw. The multi-number form and the former `id_link_c.asp` route
remain endpoint leads. Native retrieval needs a retained positive to establish
whole identity and history layout.

The carrier's [tracking guidance](https://www.e-can.com.tw/search_Goods.aspx) limits
history to two months and defines the displayed date and time as entry of the
shipment status. Empty or expired detail pages cannot confirm that a parcel never
existed. Archived samples belong in [numbers.json](numbers.json); enabled universal
providers remain the retrieval route.

Provider live-test inputs and commands are in the
[provider READMEs](../../providers/README.md).
