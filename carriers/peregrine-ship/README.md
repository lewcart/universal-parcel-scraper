# Peregrine Ship

Peregrine Ship parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Peregrine Ship (Peregrine Group Limited, Hong Kong) sources goods from Chinese factories, stores
them in its own warehouse in China and ships online shops' orders abroad, handing the last mile to
national posts and couriers such as USPS, Royal Mail, Australia Post and La Poste. Its site is
peregrineship.com.

## Numbers

Peregrine Ship publishes no number format, and no public page shows one of its tracking numbers,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its terms of service forbid automated access.

## Links

The tracker at track.peregrineship.com shows a number at `/track/f/<number>`, and its last-mile
view at `/track/l/<number>`; both name Peregrine Ship. A link holding several numbers is not read.
