# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` follow the shape of the page
`pittohio.com/myPittOhio/Shipping/LTL/TraceRequest?pro=` renders for one PRO number: the current
status, the hidden documents table, the shipment history with the terminals' pop-ups, and, on
`delivered.html`, the appointment section and the site's quick trace form. `delivered.html` has a
failed delivery attempt before the delivery. `in-transit.html` is a shipment still on its way,
modeled on a delivered page. `not-found.html` is the search form the page answers with for a
number without a shipment, with its notice and the number in its first field. PRO numbers, places,
terminals, addresses, phone numbers and clocks are invented, and the site around the tracking is
cut down.
