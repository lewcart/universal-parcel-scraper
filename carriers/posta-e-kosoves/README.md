# Posta e Kosovës

Posta e Kosovës parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Posta e Kosovës (Kosovo Post), a public company owned by the Government of Kosovo, carries letters
and parcels and offers financial services through its post offices across Kosovo. Its site is
postakosoves.com.

## Numbers

Its international items carry UPU S10 numbers, and its about page names XZ as Kosovo's temporary UPU
code. Generic postal detection handles S10 numbers, so no rule for Posta e Kosovës claims them.
Domestic registered items get an identifier whose layout it does not publish.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking box on its home page posts the number with a WordPress nonce from the page, and the
site asks the post's counter system, which answers with an HTML table; an unknown number gets an
empty table. No public number has shown a known item's reply yet.

## Links

The tracking box posts the number and has no address for one item, so there are no tracking links.
