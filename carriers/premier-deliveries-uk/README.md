# Premier Deliveries

Premier Deliveries parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Premier Deliveries, in Tipton in the West Midlands, delivers furniture and other bulky goods to
homes across the UK for retailers, with two-person crews. Its site is premierdeliveries.co.uk.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a consignment number of at least five characters and gives no layout or example.
No public number is known, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the delivery postcode.

## Links

The tracker posts the number and postcode from the form on its home page, so there are no tracking
links.
