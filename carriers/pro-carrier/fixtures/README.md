# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` follow the tracker's page for one parcel: the search
field holding the number asked, the status block with the partner button, the Shipment Details
list and the Shipment History, newest first, each scan with its comments below it.

`delivered.html` goes from the retailer's data through customs to a delivery by Colis Privé,
whose button carries a longer reference in its query. One scan is repeated, an email notice
sits between two scans and the delivery's comment names who signed and a phone.
`in-transit.html` has left Pro Carrier's hub for InPost in the UK, and one of its event names
is not in the map. `not-found.html` is the page for a number the tracker does not know.

Numbers, names, phones, places and clocks are invented.
