# Product Care Group

Product Care Group parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Product Care Group, in Hindley Green near Wigan, whose companies include Product Care Services
Limited, delivers large appliances to homes across mainland Britain with two-person crews, for
appliance makers such as BSH and for retailers, and sells appliances under its own and licensed
brands. Its site is productcaregroup.com.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes an order reference and gives no layout or example. No public reference is known,
so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the delivery postcode as well as the order reference.

## Links

Lookups need the postcode, so there are no tracking links.
