# PTS Worldwide Express

PTS Worldwide Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

PTS Worldwide Express, known to customers as PTS Kargo, is a Turkish courier for international
express and domestic e-commerce parcels. Its waybills have ten digits: archived lookups on its
trackers and customer complaints show them. Complaints about Nike orders it delivered also
quote fourteen-digit numbers starting 9001.

No rule claims PTS numbers. Ten and fourteen digits are lengths many carriers share, and the
9001 numbers have only been seen with one shipper.

## Links

The current tracking page posts the number in a form, so no address opens one parcel. The
former tracker took the number in the `trackid` parameter of `/sqlptstrack.php` on pts.net;
archived lookups show it, and the library reads the number from such addresses.
