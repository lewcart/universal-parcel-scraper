# PXC Pacific Global

PXC Pacific Global parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

PXC Pacific Global Private Limited, in Mumbai, is an international courier and freight forwarder and
an IATA agent, with a UK arm, Pacific Express Corporation, near London. Its site is pacificexp.net.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its AWB numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page asks an open JSON service with a login its page gives every visitor. The service
answers an unknown number with a not-found code, and its result names the carrier that delivers the
last leg, with that carrier's number and a link to its tracker. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `awbno` on the tracking page, which takes several numbers
separated by commas.
