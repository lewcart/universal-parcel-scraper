# Rivigo

Rivigo parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Rivigo is a B2B express network in India. Mahindra Logistics bought the business and its brand,
and runs it as Rivigo by Mahindra Logistics on the same tracker.

## Numbers

Rivigo consignment numbers are ten digits, as customers quote them in complaints. No rule
claims them, since many carriers print ten-digit runs.

## Links

The Zoom tracker opens one consignment at `zoom.rivigo.com/#/trackconsignment/` followed by the
number. The route works without a login, while the rest of Zoom is the staff panel.
