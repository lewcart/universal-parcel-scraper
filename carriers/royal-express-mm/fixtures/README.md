# Fixtures

The pages of `https://royalx.net/tracking`, cut down to the head, the tracking forms and the
answer, with the markup the tracker serves. `page.html` is the empty form with its token.
`delivered.html` is the answer for a branch waybill picked up in Yangon and delivered in
Mandalay after a failed delivery, with the sender, receiver and recipient, and one scan the page
repeats. `in-transit.html` is the answer for a member waybill still in Yangon, shown without its
sender or receiver as the tracker shows some parcels. `not-found.html` is the line the tracker
gives a waybill it does not know.

Waybills, tokens, names, office codes, townships and clocks are invented.
