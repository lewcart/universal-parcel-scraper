# Fixtures

The pages follow `trackeng.asp` in English, its scripts and notices left out. `delivered.html`
goes from the origin station abroad to the delivery, with every row the English page shows: the
flight's expected arrival among the scans, one repeated flight scan, a failed attempt, the
delivery with its `[STAMP]`, Sagawa Express's scans under the destination station in bold, and
the footer that names the Sagawa Express waybill. `in-transit.html` waits in customs, before the
footer appears, with no reference or destination station. `not-found.html` is the page for an
air waybill the tracker does not know, and `list.html` the list it shows when a lookup matches
several records.

Numbers, references, places and clocks are invented.
