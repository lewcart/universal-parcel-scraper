# Seabourne Logistics

Seabourne Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Seabourne Logistics, run by Seabourne Group Limited in Staines, founded in 1962 and owned by Santova
since 2025, ships parcels and mail abroad from offices in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and
France, and handles customs, freight forwarding and fulfilment. Its former South African company is
now separate. Its site is seabournelogistics.com.

## Numbers

Its own air waybills are nine digits starting with 20, a shape other carriers' rules read, so there
is no rule. It hands the last leg to other carriers, whose numbers its tracker shows as
cross-references; those belong to the carriers that issued them.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker posts the number with an anti-forgery token and cookie from its page, without a login or
CAPTCHA, and says when it finds no shipment. No public number it still knows has been found. An
adapter can follow.

## Links

The tracker takes the number only from its form, so there are no tracking links.
