# Fixtures

The pages follow what `sendex.coordi.com/rastreo/` renders after its form posts one guía,
without most of the site's theme: the results widget, then the tracking form.

- `delivered.html` uses the short labels: received at the origin branch, transit through an
  operations centre, one movement listed twice, the delivery round and the delivery. The card
  names the recipient, the destination and who received the parcel; the commitment date is
  the page's dash.
- `in-transit.html` uses the older sentences, from the pickup to a failed attempt and a new
  delivery round, with a commitment date.
- `not-found.html` is the notice for a guía the tracker does not hold.

Guías, names, offices, places, postcodes and clocks are invented.
