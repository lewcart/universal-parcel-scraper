# Senyu International

Senyu International parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Senyu International (森屿国际, Swift Span in its logo) is a Shenzhen freight forwarder for air,
sea, land and express shipments, with sister companies in the United States. Its site is
senyugg.cn.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracking page is its own page on the NextSLS forwarder platform. No public number has been
found to check a found reply with.

## Links

The tracking page opens from a search form; no shipment link format is known, so links are not
read.
