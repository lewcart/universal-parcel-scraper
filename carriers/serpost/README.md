# Serpost

Serpost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

Serpost, Peru's national post, tracks items by their UPU S10 number: two letters, nine digits
and two letters. Its tracking page accepts only that layout, for domestic and international
items. Items posted in Peru end in PE; inbound items keep the origin post's suffix. The
shared international postal rule recognizes the layout, so Serpost adds no rule of its own.

## Links

Serpost has no tracking address that opens a shipment; its tracking page looks the number up
from a script.
