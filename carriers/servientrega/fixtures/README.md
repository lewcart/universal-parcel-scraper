# Fixtures

The replies follow the `ControlRastreovalidaciones` lookup for one guide: an envelope with the
step the tracker reached and one result, the guide's record with its overall state and its
movements, oldest first. `delivered.json` is a delivery reported and then verified, with a
movement repeated, one logged after the delivery, a proof-of-delivery picture and a signatory.
`in-transit.json` is a guide on its way back after a wrong address, with a return notice and a
customer-service task. `returned.json` is a guide delivered back to its sender after two
reasons logged at once. `not-found.json` is the reply for a guide Servientrega does not know.

Numbers, names, addresses, contacts, towns, amounts and clocks are invented; the picture is a
single blank pixel.
