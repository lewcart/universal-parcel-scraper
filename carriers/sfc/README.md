# SFC Service

SFC Service parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

SFC (Sendfromchina, 三态) is a Chinese cross-border e-commerce logistics company. Its parcels are
handed to postal and courier partners in the destination country.

## Numbers

Customers quote SFC numbers: `SFC`, a digit, two letters and thirteen or fourteen digits, which
`sfc-1` claims. The tracker also takes the partner's tracking number and the shop's reference.

## Links

The tracking form posts the numbers, so no address opens one parcel and no link is read.
