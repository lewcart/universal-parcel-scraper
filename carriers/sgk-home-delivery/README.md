# SGK Home Delivery

SGK Home Delivery parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

SGK Home Delivery, the trading name of SGK Distribution Ltd in Bilston in the West Midlands,
delivers furniture, beds and flooring to homes across the UK with two-person crews, with assembly
and collections. Its site is sgkhomedelivery.co.uk.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a tracking id of letters and digits and gives no layout or example. No public id
is known, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the delivery postcode.

## Links

The tracking page sends the id and postcode from its form to its delivery platform, so there are no
tracking links.
