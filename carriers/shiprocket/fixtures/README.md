# Fixtures

Synthetic pages of Shiprocket's tracker, built on its markup. `in-transit.html` is a
ShiprocketX parcel on its way to the export hub: the order's row at midnight, the domestic
courier's hubs with their states, Shiprocket's hub rows in Delhi and an unplaced departure.
`delivered-abroad.html` is a ShiprocketX parcel delivered in the United States: the domestic
leg, the export hub, a flight, a customs row at midnight, USPS's pre-advice and scans, and an
order row placed at the buyer's town. `archived.html` is the archived view of an older
shipment, `not-found.html` the HTTP 400 page for an unknown AWB and `estimate.json` the
delivery estimate the page asks for.

AWBs, order ids, merchants, places, tokens and dates are invented.
