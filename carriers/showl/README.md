# Showl

Showl parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Showl (森鸿国际物流), based in Shenzhen, runs cash-on-delivery parcel lines for online sellers to Southeast
Asia, Japan, Europe and the Gulf. Its site is showl.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its tracker on oms.showl.com posts the number to `Tracking/Trace` without a login and shows the reply in
place. No public number is known to see a found reply.

## Links

Its tracker looks numbers up in place, so no address names one.
