# SkladUSA

SkladUSA parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

SkladUSA, run by Ukrainian Treasures Inc, sends Ukrainian sellers' orders to buyers in the United
States, consolidated or by express, and fulfils orders for Amazon and other marketplaces, with
offices in Kyiv and Riga and warehouses in Minnesota and Łódź. Etsy lists it as a carrier. Its site
is skladusa.com.

## Numbers

Its system tracking number covers the whole route, from Kyiv to delivery by UPS, FedEx, DHL or USPS
in the United States. The two in its knowledge base are SK, ten digits and UA. They come from one
source, so `skladusa-1` suggests SkladUSA at low confidence. Its API's placeholder has eleven
digits, which the rule does not take.

## Why there is no adapter

Its user agreement forbids automated access.
