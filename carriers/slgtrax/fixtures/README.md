# Fixtures

The replies follow `/api/shipment/track/consignee/public`, the lookup slgtrax.com's tracking box
makes, with the shipper, pickup, consignee and order fields it carries. `delivered.json` is a
cash-on-delivery parcel picked up by a rider, with a failed attempt and its reason before the
delivery and one scan listed twice. `in-transit.json` was dropped at the origin office and has
reached the destination city. `not-found.json` is the answer for a number Trax has no shipment
under.

Numbers, ids, names, phone numbers, addresses, cities, orders, amounts and clocks are invented.
