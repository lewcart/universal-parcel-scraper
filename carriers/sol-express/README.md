# Sol Express

Sol Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Sol Express (赛洛快运), a freight forwarder registered in Hong Kong, sends air, sea and express
shipments from China to Israel, duty paid or not, and keeps a warehouse in Israel, where couriers
such as e-Cargo deliver the last leg. Its site is solexpress.cn and its tracker, SOLTRACK, is at
sol-track.com.

## Numbers

Its shipping policy says every shipment gets a number made of ECSE and eight digits. No public
number is known, and e-Cargo, its Israeli last-mile partner, also gives numbers starting with EC,
so no rule claims the layout.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker shows a number's statuses on a page of its own without a session, but answers an
unknown number with an empty list rather than an error. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as the path on sol-track.com.
