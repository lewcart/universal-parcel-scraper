# Fixtures

The pages follow the tracker's page for one number: the form, then a summary naming the item and
its scans newest first, each with its type, date, time, branch and comment. `delivered.html` is a
domestic item from its acceptance to its collection at the office that sent the notice, with one
dispatch listed twice and the name of the person who collected it in the summary and in the
delivery's comment. `in-transit.html` is mail from abroad held by customs for an invoice, with
two scans at the same minute and without a branch. `not-found.html` is the page's notice for a
number its records do not hold.

Numbers, names, offices and clocks are invented.
