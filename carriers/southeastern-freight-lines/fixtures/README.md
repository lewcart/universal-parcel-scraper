# Fixtures

Replies of the Web Connect tracing service in its XML form, without a MySEFL login.

`delivered.xml` goes from the pickup to the delivery through a hub, with the origin's scans in
another zone than the destination's; one arrival is repeated, and the delivery scan names its
signer. `in-transit.xml` waits at the delivering service center for an appointment the status
names, with a pickup coded as loaded on trailer. Both carry a shipper's and consignee's town and
ZIP code, the service centers' phones, pieces and a weight. `not-found.xml` is the answer for a
PRO SEFL does not know.

Numbers, places, people, trailers, phones and clocks are invented.
