# Fixtures

`delivered.json` and `in-transit.json` follow the reply of `listExpressTrack` for one number,
newest scan first, with the fields the tracker sends for each scan. `delivered.json` crosses from
the Chinese warehouse to a South African pickup point. Its pickup, delivery and signature scans
name people and a phone number as the tracker words them, one scan is repeated, one carries
the action the page hides, and an issue scan gives a rescheduled delivery's new day. Its duty-wait scan comes after the flight departure although its wall
clock reads earlier. `not-found.json` is the answer for a number Speedaf does not know.

Numbers, names, phone numbers, sites and clocks are invented.
