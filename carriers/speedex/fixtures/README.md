# Fixtures

The files follow Speedex's tracker page, `NewTrackAndTrace.aspx`, in Greek, oldest scan first,
with the page's search field echoing the number. `delivered.html` goes from a voucher waiting
for the sender to a delivery card naming who took the parcel. Shop scans carry a street
address, a phone number and a map link, one routing is repeated, a shop scan is cancelled
between two routings, a message to the recipient comes before the delivery, and one scan writes
ΑΠΟ with its accent. `in-transit.html` has a failed message, a missed delivery and a day held at
the branch. `returned.html` is a DHL number Speedex sends back, with three scans in one minute.
`not-found.html` is the "no results" notice. The page's view state holds placeholder data.

Numbers, names, addresses, phones, places and clocks are invented.
