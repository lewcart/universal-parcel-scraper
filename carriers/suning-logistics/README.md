# Suning Logistics

Suning Logistics parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Suning Logistics (苏宁物流), the logistics arm of the Suning retail group, stores and delivers parcels and
large appliances across China, with installation. Its site is wuliu.suning.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Its waybill query asks for a slider CAPTCHA and posts the numbers with a token. No public number is known to
see a found reply.

## Links

Its waybill query posts its form, so no address names a number.
