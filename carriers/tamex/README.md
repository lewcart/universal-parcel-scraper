# Tamex

Tamex parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Tamex (تاميكس) delivers the last mile of online shops' orders in Saudi Arabia and stores and handles
stock for them. Its site is tamex.com.sa.

## Numbers

Its numbers are fourteen digits, starting with 1 in older numbers and with 3 since 2024. Fourteen
digits starting with 3 are a shape that many other carriers' rules and samples also read, so no rule
claims it.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker is a plain page that lists a parcel's statuses in Arabic, dated by day without times,
without a session or CAPTCHA. It answers a number whose history it no longer keeps, such as an older
one, the same way as an unknown number. An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number in the path after `/track/`.
