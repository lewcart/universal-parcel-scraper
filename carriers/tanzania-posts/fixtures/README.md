# Fixtures

`delivered.html` is the result panel the lookup returns in `temp` for an item posted at one
Tanzanian office and delivered at another: the sender and the addressee with their phone numbers
and addresses, the barcode, the origin and destination regions with their offices, the item's
kind, weight and status, who took delivery with their phone number, identity document and the
delivery day, and the scans oldest first, one listed twice. `in-transit.html` is the panel of an
item still on its way, without the delivery's table; its status wording is invented, as the
tracker's wording for an item on its way was not seen.

`not-found.json` is the lookup's reply for a code the tracker does not hold, and `unpaid.json`
its reply for an item whose charge is unpaid, with the amount and the payment's control number.

Numbers, names, phone numbers, addresses, offices, weights and clocks are invented.
