# Fixtures

The pages follow `www.tciexpress.in/track-your-shipment?trackshipment={docket}&dwb=dwb`: the
docket's number, status line, pin code and location, then its tables. `delivered.html` has
both delivery runs, the first one failed, its booking with a phone number after the delivery
station, three legs and the proof-of-delivery button. `in-transit.html` has its booking, with
a note after the delivery station, and two legs, the last one arrived. `not-found.html` is the
empty result for a docket the tracker does not know. `old-docket.html` keeps only its delivery
rows, without the docket number.

Numbers, phone numbers, pin codes, stations and clocks are invented.
