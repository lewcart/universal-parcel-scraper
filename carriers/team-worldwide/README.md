# Team Worldwide

Team Worldwide shipments use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Team Worldwide is a US air and ground freight carrier with branch offices and its own truck
fleet. Its Team Trac page shows a shipment's status and, for deliveries by appointment, when
the terminal is scheduling the delivery.

## Numbers

Archived Team Trac lookups show Team bill numbers in two layouts: a three-letter station code
and six digits, and a two-digit year, a station code, a letter and six digits. Few numbers are
known and station codes are airport codes other carriers print too, so no rule claims them.

## Links

Archived Team Trac lookups open one shipment at
`www.teamww.com/wp-content/tracking/teamTrac.php?hbl={number}`. An older results page took the
same `hbl` parameter. The library reads the number from both.
