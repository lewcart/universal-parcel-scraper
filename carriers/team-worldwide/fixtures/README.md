# Fixtures

Each file follows the page `www.teamww.com/tracking/team-trac` returns to its form, trimmed to
the page title, the answer and the form, with each bill's answer in its own block.

`delivered.html` goes from the shipper's notification to the delivery, with a pickup
appointment, a repeated row, a delivery appointment naming its booking agent, a station remark,
towns after the pickup and out-for-delivery rows, and the header naming the signer.
`in-transit.html` is at the destination station after its recovery, with the header's requested
delivery day and the page's note on booking the delivery. `not-found.html` is the notice for a
bill Team Trac has no status for.

Bills, references, towns, ZIP codes, signers, agents, remarks, phone numbers and clocks are
invented.
