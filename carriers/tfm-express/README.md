# TFM Express

TFM Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

## Numbers

TFM Express is a Dubai courier for the UAE, the GCC and Iraq. Its airwaybills are twelve
digits; two sets of shipping labels a sender published, headed with TFM Express's name and the
airwaybill number, give them. Twelve-digit runs are shared with FedEx and other carriers, so no
rule claims them and the gap is declared.

## Links

The tracker on `customer.tfmex.com/Skybill/TrackSkybill` posts the airwaybill from its form
with an anti-forgery field and shows the history in place. No address opens one parcel.
