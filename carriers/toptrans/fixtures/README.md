# Fixtures

`delivered.html` and `in-transit.html` follow the page TOPTRANS's tracking service renders for a
shipment: the shipment's fields, then its scans, oldest first. `delivered.html` is a three-piece
winter shipment delivered on a second round, with both drivers, the driver's message, who took
it, a reference, a note and cash on delivery filled in. `in-transit.html` is a one-piece summer
shipment at its delivery depot. Both have a row without a depot. `not-found.html` is the page for
a number the service does not know.

Numbers, references, people, phone numbers, depots, towns, amounts and clocks are invented.
