# Fixtures

`delivered.html`, `in-transit.html`, `pickup.html` and `not-found.html` follow the shape of
the tracing results page of TST-CF Express for one PRO looked up through its URL tracing
address, trimmed to the results table. `delivered.html` is freight delivered in the United
States with its Saia shipment ID, appointment and customs release; `in-transit.html` freight
between two Canadian terminals whose estimate is a note to call; `pickup.html` a completed
pickup whose status names the PRO it was given; `not-found.html` the answer for a PRO the
tracing service does not have. PROs, partner IDs, places, references, weights, phone numbers and
clocks are invented.
