# Union Express

Union Express parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Union Express (联合运通), a freight forwarder in Shenzhen that also trades as CTG, sends express,
air, sea and small-parcel shipments from China to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom
and Europe, including stock for Amazon's warehouses, and resells UPS, DHL and FedEx. Its site is
szlhyt.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its waybill numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The waybill query asks a JSON service with the page's session cookie and CSRF token, without a
login or CAPTCHA. No public number has shown a known shipment's reply yet.

## Links

The waybill query shows its answer in place, so there are no tracking links.
