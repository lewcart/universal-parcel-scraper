# United Worldwide Couriers

United Worldwide Couriers parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated
adapter.

United Worldwide Couriers is an international courier based in New Delhi, India. It
forwards shipments through a partner such as UPS and shows the partner's number next to its
own.

## Numbers

A lookup archived from its former tracker shows a seven-digit air waybill. Seven digits are
printed by many carriers, so no rule claims them.

## Links

The current tracker posts its form, so no address opens one shipment. The former tracker
took the number as `afno` on `TrackDetails.asp`.
