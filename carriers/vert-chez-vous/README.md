# Vert Chez Vous

Vert Chez Vous parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Vert Chez Vous, Labatut Group's urban delivery line, delivers in French cities by cargo bike and by
electric or natural-gas van, from depots near Paris, Lyon and Marseille. Its site vertchezvous.com
shows the group's site, apart from its tracking page.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes a delivery note number or the sender's or recipient's reference. The page's
example is a letter and nine digits, but references can be free-form and no public one is known, so
there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Every lookup needs the delivery postcode.

## Links

The tracker posts the reference and postcode from its form, so there are no tracking links.
