# Fixtures

Replies of `Consultas/RastreoGuia`, in the API's shape: fixed-width text fields padded with
spaces and the rows newest first. `not-found.json` is the reply for a number Vimenpaq does not
know: the guide's record left empty. `delivered.json` repeats its pickup row within seconds and
ends on a delivery comment that names who took the parcel and their identity card.
`in-transit.json` ends on the way to the branch.

Numbers, names, identity cards, comments and clocks are invented.
