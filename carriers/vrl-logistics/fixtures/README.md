# Fixtures

The pages follow the answer of `POST www.vrlgroup.in/Tracking/TrackShipment`, trimmed to the
form, the error toast and the result cards: the details card, then the transit card with its
current status and the rail of transit rows in its wide-screen and phone layouts, then the
hidden sample cards every page carries. Clocks keep the page's month number after the hour.
`delivered.html` is an office delivery with two rows the same day at one hub. `in-transit.html`
is a door delivery between branches, its destination still ahead. `not-found.html` is the form
with the `No data found` toast the tracker gives an LR number it does not know.

LR numbers, branches, branch codes, towns, clocks and the support number are invented.
