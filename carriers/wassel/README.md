# Wassel

Wassel parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Wassel Logistics (واصل), the brand of the Palestinian Distribution and Logistics Services company in
Ramallah, ships parcels within Palestine and abroad, offers warehousing and customs clearance, and
delivers Jordanian passports. Its site is wassel.ps.

## Numbers

Its tracker sends twelve-digit numbers starting with 88 and ten-digit numbers starting with 500 to
Wassel's own service, and DHL and FedEx numbers to those carriers. No public Wassel number is known,
and other carriers' rules read both shapes, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

Wassel's own service answers without a session or CAPTCHA, but gives a server error for an unknown
number rather than not found. No public number has shown a known shipment's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `awb` on the English or Arabic tracking page.
