# WePost

WePost parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

WePost (全民代运), run by WePost Sdn Bhd in Kulai, Johor, consolidates orders bought from Chinese
online shops and ships them to Malaysia and Singapore, where partners such as Line Clear deliver
them. Its site is wepost.com.my.

## Numbers

Its tracker takes its own numbers and the Chinese couriers' numbers of the parcels it receives,
so many numbers its customers hold belong to other carriers. No public page shows one of its
own, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads numbers from its address and draws its answer on the server without a
session. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `nums` on the tracking page.
