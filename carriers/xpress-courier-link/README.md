# ECL Express Courier Link

ECL Express Courier Link parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

ECL Express Courier Link, also written Xpress Courier Link, is a courier founded in Karachi in 1992,
with offices in Lahore and Islamabad, that carries documents and parcels within Pakistan and abroad.
Its site is xpresscourierlink.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its tracking numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker asks whether a shipment is international or domestic and shows its scans on a plain page
without a login, along with the sender's and recipient's names. No public number has shown a known
parcel's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `search` on the tracking page, with `txtstatus` naming the
international or domestic service. The tracking address opens international shipments, the form's
default.
