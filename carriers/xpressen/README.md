# Xpressen

Xpressen parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Xpressen ApS, in Herning, runs transport, courier and distribution in Denmark, including home
deliveries for webshops and large items carried by two people. Its site is xpressen.dk.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its parcel numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracking page reads the number from its address and draws its answer, with the delivery
photo, on the server without a session. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply yet.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `t` on the tracking page.
