# Yingtu Supply Chain

Yingtu Supply Chain parcels use enabled universal providers. There is no dedicated adapter.

Yingtu Supply Chain (盈途供应链), based in Guangzhou, flies online orders from mainland China and
Hong Kong to Los Angeles and New York on its US small-parcel line, where USPS delivers them, and
runs a European small-parcel line and a US warehouse. Its site is yingtu56.com.

## Numbers

No public page shows one of its numbers or states their layout, so there is no rule.

## Why there is no adapter

The tracker reads numbers from its address and draws its answer on the server without a session,
leaving the fields empty for an unknown number. No public number has shown a known parcel's reply
yet. An adapter can follow.

## Links

Tracking links carry the number as `documentCode` on the tracker.
